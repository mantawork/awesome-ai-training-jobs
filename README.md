# Awesome AI Training Jobs [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re)

> Platforms that pay people to train, test and label data for AI, A to Z: what the work is, how you get in, and the open roles and pay their own listings post this week.

AI training work means rating and writing answers for AI models, coding and math tasks, translation, voice recording and data labeling, usually remote and paid by the hour. This list has 58 platforms. 31 post their roles publicly, with 3,198 open roles this week. Counts and pay come from [MantaWork](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs), which reads each platform's own listings every day.

Updated weekly. Last update: 2026-11-17.

## Platforms that post open roles

- [Alignerr](https://www.alignerr.com/en/jobs) - Owned by Labelbox. 450+ active roles across STEM, coding, audio, language, and general categories. Public role listings; you apply per role. 24 open roles this week, $15 to $100/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/alignerr).
- [Anyone AI](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/anyone-ai) - Hires experts in math, science, finance and software to train AI. Most roles list an hourly rate and a country. 212 open roles this week, $15 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/anyoneai).
- [Babel Audio](https://dashboard.babel.audio/sign-up) - Collects voice data for AI models: transcription, voice acting and recorded conversations, in many languages. Freelance and fully online. Pay is per hour of finished audio, not per hour you work. Each listing names its language and who can apply. 53 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/babel).
- [Braintrust](https://app.usebraintrust.com/jobs/) - A talent marketplace that re-lists contract roles from other companies. Only AI training and data roles are shown here. Each listing names the employer. 41 open roles this week, $10 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/braintrust).
- [Chemin](https://apply.workable.com/chemin/) - Posts AI data projects such as recording, transcription, translation review and annotation. Most are remote, but each is tied to one country or language, often Malaysia, Hong Kong, Singapore or India. 20 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/chemin).
- [Claru](https://claru.ai/jobs) - Get paid per hour in USD to film everyday tasks: chores, cooking, gardening, and work in shops, offices and warehouses. Use a phone or a head mount. Each project lists its countries. Claru also has game data and annotation projects. 48 open roles this week, $5 to $50/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/claru).
- [CloudFactory](https://apply.workable.com/cloudfactory/) - An AI data company. It hires part-time workers to record video and site data for AI, often in person at construction sites in one city. It also hires bilingual computational linguists. Check the place and hours on each posting. 17 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/cloudfactory).
- [CrowdGen by Appen](https://jobs.lever.co/appen) - Data collection, transcription and evaluation projects. Check each project for its language, location and payment terms. 16 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/crowdgen).
- [DataForce by TransPerfect](https://community.transperfect.com/jobs) - Data collection and annotation projects, including participant tasks and freelance projects. Check the work location and engagement terms in each listing. 165 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/dataforce).
- [iMerit Scholars](https://scholars.net/en/jobs) - iMerit's platform for paid AI tasks. Roles include language specialists, audio and video review, and expert summaries. Each role lists the countries it is open to. The site states pay from $40 an hour overall, but it gives no rate per role. 44 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/scholars).
- [Innodata](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/innodatainc) - A data engineering company listed on NASDAQ. It hires part-time AI associates, annotators and evaluators, mostly with flexible hours. Many roles are open in one US state or one country. 29 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/innodata).
- [Kastle](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/Kastle) - A Y Combinator company that builds AI voice agents for US mortgage lenders. Hires part-time AI trainers. You need mortgage call-center experience. 1 open role this week.
- [Label Your Data](https://apply.workable.com/labelyourdata/) - A data annotation company. Its current roles are based in Ukraine. 1 open role this week.
- [Lilt](https://jobs.ashbyhq.com/lilt-production) - A language AI company. Its LiltLancer community hires AI training contributors, voice talent and subject matter experts. They build and score AI test tasks in their own language. 378 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/lilt).
- [LXT](https://lxt.bamboohr.com/careers) - An AI data company. It hires freelancers for speech recording, audio data collection and language review. Each project names its languages and countries. 15 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/lxt).
- [Mercor](https://work.mercor.com/) - AI-interview-based talent network. One application, voice interview with their AI, then matched to projects across coding, research, and specialist work. Pay scales with track and seniority. 327 open roles this week, $4.56 to $500/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/mercor).
- [Meridial](https://job-boards.eu.greenhouse.io/agency) - The marketplace run by Invisible Technologies for AI training projects. The same project is posted once per country, so look for the listing in your country. 544 open roles this week, $6 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/meridial).
- [micro1](https://www.micro1.ai/experts/opportunities) - Recruits experts and generalists for AI training projects and lists each role with an hourly range on its own job board. You apply per role, then do a role-specific interview with micro1's AI recruiter. 325 open roles this week, $9 to $400/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/micro1).
- [Mindrift](https://mindrift.ai/apply) - Remote AI training platform with category-organized tracks (code, STEM, design, writing, legal, consulting). Published pay band $15-$100/hr. Lower friction than Mercor or Alignerr; project supply has been growing through 2026. 22 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/mindrift).
- [OneForma (Centific)](https://www.oneforma.com/jobs/) - OneForma is the contributor marketplace for Centific (formerly Pactera EDGE). Hires for domain-specific projects: cultural annotation, healthcare data, video data collection, photo ML evaluation. Mostly task-based pay rather than hourly. 16 open roles this week, $5 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/oneforma).
- [OpenTrain](https://www.opentrain.ai/jobs/) - A marketplace where AI teams post paid data work. Data collection jobs include at-home video recording, first-person chore footage and 360-camera vlogging. Most pay per hour of approved video. Each job lists its pay and countries. 41 open roles this week, $6 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/opentrain).
- [Outlier](https://outlier.ai/) - Operated by Scale AI. The largest AI training marketplace by contributor count. Multiple project tracks (coding, language, math, science). Pay varies widely by track and project. 10 open roles this week, $18 to $80/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/outlier).
- [Productive Playhouse](https://productiveplayhouse.applytojob.com/) - A language data company. It hires AI product evaluators, transcribers and data collectors in many languages. Most roles are remote and temporary. 49 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/productiveplayhouse).
- [Remo Experts](https://www.rex.zone/open-opportunities) - Lists AI data projects: speech recording, audio review, coding and expert tasks. Each project states its pay and countries. Some pay per hour of accepted audio, not per hour worked. 35 open roles this week, $20 to $40/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/remo).
- [RWS TrainAI](https://www.rws.com/artificial-intelligence/train-ai-data-services/trainai-community/) - TrainAI is the contributor arm of RWS, a publicly-listed language services company. Hires globally for AI training across most major languages. Late payment is the most common complaint in reviews. Hires via Lever job board. 126 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/rws).
- [Terac](https://terac.com/join) - Pays experts and everyday users for AI evaluation, model review, transcription, voice recording and data annotation projects, plus paid research studies. Each opportunity lists its hourly rate and who it is looking for. Fully online. 18 open roles this week, $4 to $150/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/terac).
- [Turing](https://work.turing.com/) - Hires contractors to train and test AI models for AI labs, in coding, math, science, medicine, languages and more. Most work is remote. 292 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/turing).
- [Vetto](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/vetto) - Hires expert reviewers to improve the scenarios used to train AI models. Most projects are remote and open to applicants worldwide. 20 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/vetto).
- [Welocalize](https://www.welocalize.com/welocalize-talent-community/) - Language QA at scale. Translation evaluation, speech labeling, multilingual prompt review. Pay is hourly and predictable; project supply is steady for working language pairs. 269 open roles this week, $2.25 to $37/hr in listings. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/welocalize).
- [xAI](https://job-boards.greenhouse.io/xai) - The company behind Grok. Hires AI Tutors to label, record and review data for its models, listed one role at a time on its own job board. Recent postings name the company SpaceXSI. Each listing names the countries it hires in, so check yours before you apply. 20 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/xai).
- [Yandex](https://ai-trainers.ya.ru/vacancies) - Hires AI trainers to review and improve the answers of its AI, in medicine, law, finance and languages. Postings are in Russian and pay is in roubles. 20 open roles this week. [Open roles](https://mantawork.com/ai-training-jobs/platform/yandex).

## Platforms with no public role posts

These take one application, run a task app or post gigs on a marketplace.

- [AfterQuery](https://www.afterquery.com/) - Expert tasks that build training data for frontier AI labs. How you get in: Apply on its site. Where: Worldwide.
- [Amazon Mechanical Turk](https://www.mturk.com/) - Short online tasks, including AI data labelling. How you get in: Sign up as a worker, then pick tasks. Where: Worldwide, US.
- [Awign](https://www.awign.com/) - Head-camera video of home and work tasks for robot training, plus annotation. How you get in: Sign up for gig work on its site. Where: India.
- [ByteDance Xpert](https://xpert.bytedance.com/) - Chinese-speaking experts review and write AI training data. Tasks arrive by skill tag. How you get in: Sign up by phone, then pass a one-hour entrance exam. Where: China.
- [Clickworker](https://www.clickworker.com/) - Short online tasks: text, photo and audio data for AI. How you get in: Sign up on the site or app. Where: Worldwide, Europe.
- [CrowdWorks (Japan)](https://crowdworks.jp/) - Japanese annotation and AI evaluation gigs posted by clients. How you get in: Create a freelancer profile, then apply to each gig. Where: Japan.
- [Crowdworks (Korea)](https://crowdworks.kr/) - Korean data tasks in its app, plus project roles for speakers of Vietnamese, Malay, Thai and Khmer living in Korea. How you get in: Do tasks in the app. Project roles are posted one at a time. Where: Korea.
- [DataAnnotation](https://www.dataannotation.tech/) - Writing, coding and reasoning tasks that train chatbots. How you get in: One application with an assessment. It posts no separate roles. Where: Worldwide.
- [DataVale AI](https://www.datavaleai.com/) - First-person video of household and industrial tasks for robot training. How you get in: Apply on its site. Where: India.
- [Deccan AI Experts](https://deccanexperts.ai/) - Ranking AI answers, checking agent steps and writing prompts, in 40+ languages. How you get in: Request an invite on its site. Where: India, Worldwide.
- [Flitto Arcade](https://www.flitto.com/) - Language data tasks in 90+ languages, with rewards based on checked quality. How you get in: Sign up in Flitto Arcade. Where: Korea, Worldwide.
- [Gray Swan Arena](https://app.grayswan.ai/arena) - Contests to break AI agents safely, for prize money. How you get in: Join a contest on its arena page. Where: Worldwide.
- [Handshake AI](https://joinhandshake.com/ai/) - Paid, remote AI training work for experts and generalists, from associate degrees to PhDs. How you get in: Apply in the Handshake app. Where: US.
- [Hashlist](https://hashlist.com/) - AI tasks for embedded, hardware and controls engineers, paid per accepted task. How you get in: Apply on its site. Where: Worldwide.
- [Human Science](https://www.science.co.jp/company/partner.html) - Doctors and dentists label medical AI data; translators work in Japanese, English, Korean and Chinese. How you get in: Apply by email to each posting on its partner page. Where: Japan.
- [Karya](https://www.karya.in/) - Speech, text and evaluation tasks in 115+ languages, including Indian languages. How you get in: Join through its app and partner programmes. Where: India.
- [Lancers](https://www.lancers.jp/) - Japanese annotation and comparison gigs posted by clients. How you get in: Create a freelancer profile, then apply to each gig. Where: Japan.
- [Neevo](https://neevo.ai/) - Text, audio, image and video tasks in 500+ languages, paid by PayPal. How you get in: Sign up on its site. Where: Worldwide.
- [Perle](https://www.perle.ai/) - Transcription and audio work by language, including African languages. How you get in: Apply on its site. Where: Worldwide, Africa.
- [Prolific](https://www.prolific.com/) - Paid research studies and AI tasks. How you get in: Sign up, and studies appear in your account. Where: Worldwide, Europe, US.
- [Remotasks](https://www.remotasks.com/) - Image tagging, transcription and moderation tasks. English is the only requirement. How you get in: Sign up on its site. Where: Worldwide.
- [Respondent](https://www.respondent.io/) - Paid interviews and diary studies. How you get in: Sign up, and studies appear in your account. Where: Worldwide, US.
- [Shaip](https://collaborate.shaip.com/) - Data collection, annotation and search evaluation, including Indian languages such as Bengali and Sylheti. How you get in: Sign up on its community portal. Payouts are weekly. Where: India, Worldwide.
- [Sovrano AI](https://sovrano.ai/) - Expert evaluations and data in 23 European languages. How you get in: Apply on its site. Where: Europe.
- [TELUS Digital AI Community](https://www.telusdigital.com/careers/ai-community) - Rater and evaluator roles by language and country, such as Spanish, Arabic and Canadian French. How you get in: Apply per role on its AI Community page. Where: Worldwide, Latin America, Middle East, Europe.
- [Tencent Hunyuan ExpertHub](https://hyexpert.tencent.com/) - Chinese-speaking professionals review medical, legal, finance and coding data for Tencent's Hunyuan models. How you get in: Apply with the online form. Each task shows its rate. Where: China.
- [Toloka](https://toloka.ai/) - Annotation projects in Russian, German, English and other languages. How you get in: Apply per posting on its annotator job board. Where: Europe, Russia, Worldwide.

## How this list is made

- A platform is listed when a named company pays the worker, joining is free, the application runs on the company's own site or job board, and the work trains, tests or supplies data for AI. Closed platforms are removed.
- The order is A to Z. Every link goes to the platform's own page or to MantaWork.
- Open roles and pay are counted from each platform's own listings by [MantaWork](https://mantawork.com) and refreshed here every week.
- Know a platform that is missing? Open an issue with its name and its own jobs page.
