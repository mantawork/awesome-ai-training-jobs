# Contributing

Know an AI training or data annotation platform that is missing? Open an issue with:

- the platform's name
- its own jobs or sign-up page
- what the work is, in one line

A platform is listed when a named company pays the worker, joining is free, the application runs on the company's own site or job board, and the work trains, tests or supplies data for AI.

The list is rebuilt every week from the platforms' own listings, so hand edits to README.md are replaced at the next update. Corrections go in an issue.
